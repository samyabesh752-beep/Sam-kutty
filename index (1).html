About

about.txt

Hi, I'm Sam. My full name is G. Sam Yabesh, and I've just started my B.Tech in Information Technology.

I got into tech by wondering why a screen does what it does when you press a key. Old computers still fascinate me: chunky keyboards, beige boxes, whole worlds squeezed into a few kilobytes. That's why this site looks the way it does.

Right now I'm in the early, messy stage. I break things, read the error, and try again. I'm not an expert yet and I'm fine saying so. What I do have is patience for debugging and a real itch to understand what happens underneath.

I'd like to build things that are useful to real people, and keep my sense of humour while I do it.
